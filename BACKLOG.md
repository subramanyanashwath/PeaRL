# Backlog

Explicitly deferred beyond the PeaRL v1 core sprint:

- Bayesian A/B testing;
- generalized sequential testing and alpha-spending frameworks;
- multi-arm bandits and Bayesian optimization;
- production telemetry and distribution-shift monitoring;
- multi-judge ensembles;
- hosted or longitudinal experiment registries;
- distributed execution and multi-tenant SaaS;
- model-weight training, PPO, GRPO, RLHF, and reward-model training;
- automatic environment generation from arbitrary text;
- generative scenario agents;
- browser UI and performance dashboards;
- broad provider and plugin ecosystems;
- additional Gold Environments beyond the five specified for v1.

Items enter Days 1–14 only when they are release, correctness, or generality
blockers under the canonical PRD.
